flowchart TD
  %% Entry & Auth
  A[Start: App Launch] --> B[Login / Register]
  B --> C{Has Account?}
  C -->|No| D[Register User & Email Verification]
  C -->|Yes| E[Authenticate & Issue JWT]
  E --> F{Select Role}

  %% Role Branch
  F -->|Student| G[Student Home]
  F -->|Institute| H[Institute Dashboard]
  F -->|Admin| I[Admin Dashboard]

  %% Student Flow with Geolocation
  subgraph STUDENT_FLOW [Student Flow]
    G --> S0[Choose Search Mode]
    S0 --> S1a[Enter Area / City]
    S0 --> S1b[Use Current Location]
    S1a --> S2[Fetch Institutes WHERE address_city = input_area]
    S1b --> S3[Fetch Institutes NEARBY (lat,lon) within radius]
    S2 --> S4[List Institutes by proximity]
    S3 --> S4
    S4 --> S5[View Institute Profile]
    S5 --> S6[Write / Edit Review]
    S6 --> S7[Review Service: status=APPROVED(default for student)]
    S7 --> AdminFlow
    AdminFlow -->|Approve| S8[Review APPROVED → Visible]
    S8 --> S5
    G --> S9[My Profile & Favorites]
  end

  %% Institute Flow (with admin approval)
  subgraph INSTITUTE_FLOW [Institute Flow]
    H --> I1[Create Profile Request]
    I1 --> I2[Institute Profile Service: status=PENDING]
    I2 --> I3[Await Verification Message]
    I2 --> AdminFlow
    AdminFlow -->|Approve| I4[Profile status=APPROVED]
    AdminFlow -->|Reject| I5[Notify Institute]
    I4 --> H
    H --> I6[Edit Profile & Upload Photos]
    H --> I7[View & Respond to Reviews]
    I7 --> S8
  end

  %% Admin Flow
  subgraph ADMIN_FLOW [Admin Flow]
    I --> A1[View Institute Approval Queue]
    I --> A2[View Review Moderation Queue]
    A1 --> A3[Approve or Reject Institutes]
    A2 --> A4[Approve or Reject Reviews]
  end
